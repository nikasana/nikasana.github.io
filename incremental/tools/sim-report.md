# Economy simulation report

Generated by `tools/sim.mjs` (5 seeds per bot, 0.25s step, 120 min cap). Waits are seconds between purchases.
One-off windfalls (milestone bonuses, time capsules, finds, the Golden Needle, sky coins) are not paid in the sim: it measures the steady purchase curve.
Drift is the slope of the rolling median wait (window 21) in seconds per 100 purchases.
Growth is the effective per-level cost growth a bot felt: geometric mean of each level's price over the previous level's price of the same node (node growth, synergy reductions and purchase inflation together).
